A Christmas present for your sister-in-law that's actually personal

Type “christmas gift for sister in law” and Google suggests “who has everything” and “you don't like” – it's one of the trickier names on the list. If candles and bath sets feel like a cop-out, a story album of your year together is personal and costs nothing.

Why a story album works

  • It's about her and the family, not about guessing her taste.
  • The photos already exist – they're spread across the family group chat.
  • It's free, so it pairs nicely with a small gift or her Secret Santa present.

What to put in it

  • Family moments from the year: a summer barbecue, a birthday, a day at the seaside, Sunday roast at Mum's.
  • Photos with her kids or with your kids – the cousins together.
  • A short voice message from your partner (her brother or sister) and one from you.
  • One or two old photos, if your partner can find them.

Chapter ideas

  1. Our year as a family.
  2. The cousins.
  3. Days out.
  4. Thank you – and Merry Christmas.

How to make it (free)

On Albums you create a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters and narrated with your own voice. Albums and posts are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google or Telegram and create an album, e.g. “Christmas 2026 – for Emma”.
  2. Ask your partner and the family to send photos and voice messages.
  3. Add them chapter by chapter and write one line under each.
  4. New albums are Public by default. To keep it a surprise, set it to Only me while you work on it alone.
  5. You can add one co-author – for example your partner, once you're friends on Albums. Only me hides the album from the co-author too, so while you work together choose Friends, except… and exclude everyone else (check the list again if you add new friends).

How to give it

On Christmas Day, open it together on the TV or a tablet. To send it, switch it to Public: Public albums open in the browser without signing in, with a cover preview. Friends albums are visible only to your signed-in friends, and Only me albums don't open by link. The whole album can also be downloaded as one ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a sister-in-law who has everything?

Something personal usually works best: a story album with the year's family photos, the cousins' videos and voice messages.

Is the album free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited.

Does she need an account to see it?

Not if the album is Public: the link opens in the browser. Friends albums require her to sign in and be your friend on Albums.

Can I download the album?

Yes, the whole album can be downloaded as one ZIP file.
