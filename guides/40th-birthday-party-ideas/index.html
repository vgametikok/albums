40th birthday party ideas for a night people remember

Start typing “40th birthday party ideas” and you get “female”, “male”, “at home”, “with kids” and city names like Sydney, Perth and Brisbane. Summer is a great time for a 40th in Australia, so here are practical ideas – and an easy way to collect every guest's photos afterwards.

Choose the format

  • Backyard party: a BBQ or grazing table, fairy lights, an esky full of drinks and a playlist from the year they were born.
  • At home with kids: an afternoon start, a jumping castle or lawn games for the kids, adults stay on after dark.
  • Out: a private room at a pub, a long lunch at a winery, a rooftop bar booking.
  • Weekend away: a beach house or a country stay with close friends.

Theme ideas

  • Born in the 80s: an 80s dress-up night.
  • “40 and fabulous” or “40 and fit” for a sporty birthday person.
  • A favourite place or holiday as the theme: Bali, Italy, a footy club.
  • Cocktail party with a signature drink named after them.

Little touches

  • A board with photos from every decade of their life.
  • Short speeches from a friend, a sibling and their partner.
  • Ask guests to bring a funny or favourite memory – written down or as a voice note.

Every guest photo in one album

At a 40th, everyone takes photos on their own phone. With an event album on Albums you collect them all in one place:

  1. Create the album, e.g. “Sarah's 40th”.
  2. Put the QR code on the drinks table, the photo board or the cake table.
  3. Guests scan it with their phone camera and upload photos and videos in the browser – nothing to install.
  4. Turn on Hold guest photos if you want to approve uploads before they appear.
  5. After the party, download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Guests who upload without signing in appear anonymously. Signed-in guests also get a private draft copy with only their own uploads.
