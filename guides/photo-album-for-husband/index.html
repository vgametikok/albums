A photo album for your husband

In the UK people search “photo album for husband”, “photo album gift for husband” and “photo album for husband birthday” when a card and a bottle feel too little. A story album of your years together – photos, videos and your own voice – is personal, free and can be ready tonight.

When it works best

  • His birthday, especially a big one like 40 or 50.
  • Your wedding anniversary – one chapter for every year married.
  • Christmas morning, opened together on the sofa.
  • When he is working away and you want to send something more than a text.

What to put in

  • The first photo of you two, your first holiday, the wedding day.
  • Small everyday moments: Sunday roasts, walks with the dog, the first flat.
  • Short videos – his laugh, the first dance, the kids with their dad.
  • Voice notes from you, the children or his mates saying what he means to them.

Chapter ideas

  1. How we met.
  2. Our wedding.
  3. Home and family.
  4. Trips and holidays.
  5. Why I'd marry you again.

How to make it for free

On Albums you build a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters, and you can add voice narration. Albums are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored at up to 2048px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google and create an album, e.g. “Ten Years of Us”.
  2. Add chapters, upload photos and short clips, write a line under each.
  3. Record a voice note in the browser for the moments that need your words.
  4. You can invite one co-author, for example a sibling or one of the children; they have to be among your friends on Albums.
  5. Choose who sees it: Public, Friends or Only me. Friends means all your friends, so if he is on your friends list, use Friends except and exclude him until the big day.
  6. On the day, set it to Public and send him the link — it opens in the browser with a cover preview, nothing to install. If you set it to Friends, he must be signed in and on your friends list.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put in a photo album for your husband?

Your story together: how you met, the wedding, holidays, home life and short messages from you and the family.

Can I add voice messages?

Yes. Record voice notes in the browser and attach them to photos or chapters, or narrate the album in your own voice.

Can I keep it a surprise?

Yes. Use Friends except to hide it from him, then switch the visibility on the day.

Is it free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited.
