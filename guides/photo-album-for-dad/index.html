Photo album for Dad: a birthday or Christmas gift he will actually open

Searches for a “photo album for dad” peak around birthdays and the holidays, and most of them lead to printed photo books. If your family is spread between Toronto, Calgary and a cottage up north, an online album can do something a book cannot: hold short videos, and the voices of the kids telling him why he is their favourite. Here is how to put one together.

What to put in a photo album for Dad

  • Then and now: his old school photo next to a recent one, the first car, the first house.
  • Things he taught you: skating on the backyard rink, changing a tire, the camping trip that went sideways.
  • His people: hockey buddies, the fishing crew, colleagues from the plant or the office.
  • Grandkids’ chapter: drawings, a short video, and a voice note in their own words.
  • One caption per photo: where, when, and the line he always says about it.

Build it as a free story album

On Albums you create a story album with chapters, photos, videos and voice notes. Add narration and play it back as a slideshow. To share, add Dad as a friend and choose Friends — that includes all your friends, so to show it only to him choose Friends, except… and exclude the others. Review the setting again after you add new friends. Only me does not give access through a link. No ads. The album stays as long as you want it.

  1. Create a free album — e.g. “Dad at 60”.
  2. Add chapters: Growing up, Our family, Weekends, Grandkids.
  3. Upload photos (up to 30 MB each, up to 2048 px on the long edge on the free plan) and short videos (up to 50 MB).

  5. Invite one co-author — a sibling who is already your friend on Albums — to fill chapters with you.

Frequently asked questions

What should I put in a photo album for my dad?

Old and new photos side by side, the things he taught you, his friends, a grandkids’ chapter and a short voice message from each of you.

Can I make a photo album for Dad online for free?

Yes. Story albums on Albums are free, with chapters, photos, videos and voice notes. No ads.

How do I share it only with Dad?

Add him as a friend, choose Friends, except… and exclude everyone else. Check the setting again after adding new friends.

Can my siblings help?

You can invite one co-author per album; they need to be your friend on Albums.
