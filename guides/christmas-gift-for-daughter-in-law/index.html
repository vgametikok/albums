A Christmas gift for your daughter-in-law that says “you’re family”

Google suggests “who has everything” and “from mother” right after “christmas gift for daughter in law” – choosing for her can feel delicate. A story album about the year you spent together as a family shows that she belongs, and it costs nothing to make.

Why it works

  • It is about the family she is part of, not about guessing her taste in sweaters.
  • It works whether you are close or still getting to know each other.
  • It is free, so you can pair it with a small gift: her favourite tea, a candle or a gift card.

What to put in it

  • Family moments from the year: Thanksgiving dinner, a weekend at the cottage, a birthday, a hockey game.
  • Photos of her with your son and, if they have kids, with the grandchildren.
  • A few pictures from their wedding or the first time she came to a family dinner.
  • A short voice message from you: “We are so glad you are part of this family.”

Chapter ideas

  1. Our year together.
  2. The two of you.
  3. Family dinners and holidays.
  4. Welcome to the family – Merry Christmas.

How to make it (free)

On Albums you create a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters and narrated with your own voice. Albums and posts are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google or Telegram and create an album, e.g. “Christmas 2026 – for Sarah”.
  2. Ask your son and the rest of the family to send their photos and short clips.
  3. Add them chapter by chapter, with a one-line caption under each.
  4. New albums are Public by default. To keep it a surprise, set it to Only me while you work on it alone.
  5. You can add one co-author – for example your son, once you are friends on Albums. Only me hides the album from the co-author too, so while you work together choose Friends, except… and exclude everyone else (check the list again if you add new friends).

How to give it

Open it together on the TV or a tablet on Christmas morning. To send it, switch it to Public: Public albums open in the browser without signing in, with a cover preview. Friends albums are visible only to your signed-in friends, and Only me albums don’t open by link. The whole album can also be downloaded as one ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

What do you give a daughter-in-law who has everything?

Something personal: a story album with the year’s family photos, videos and a voice message from you.

Is the album free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited.

Does she need an account to see it?

Not if the album is Public: the link opens in the browser. Friends albums require her to sign in and be your friend on Albums.

Can she download the album?

Yes, the whole album can be downloaded as one ZIP file.
