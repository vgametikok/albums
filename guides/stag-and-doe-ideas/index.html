Stag and doe ideas: games that raise cash and one album for every guest photo

A stag and doe (stag & doe) is the Canadian wedding fundraiser that fills a hall with coworkers, cousins and friends who may never make the wedding guest list. The toonie toss and dollar dance raise money — and create photo moments that vanish into private camera rolls. Here are stag and doe ideas that work in Ontario, Alberta and beyond, plus a simple way to keep every guest photo in one place.

Classic games guests actually play

  • Toonie toss: coins toward a bottle or prize — crowds form every round.
  • Dollar dance: a short dance with the couple for a loonie or toonie; the line is full of candid photos.
  • 50/50 draw: half the pot to a winner, half to the couple — announce it loud so someone captures the cheer.
  • Silent auction / raffle tables: donated baskets and gift cards keep people moving between games.
  • Photo bingo: cards with moments to capture (toonie toss winner, first pie, dance floor) so guests hunt for pictures instead of only scrolling.

Where the best photos happen

Doors, game tables, the dollar dance line and last call on the dance floor. Hired coverage often misses the late, loud part of the night — that is exactly when guests take the funniest videos.

Frequently asked questions

What is a stag and doe?

A Canadian wedding fundraiser — usually a ticketed hall night with games, a cash bar and dancing that raises money for the couple.

What are good stag and doe ideas?

Toonie toss, dollar dance, 50/50, raffles, photo bingo and a QR album so every guest's photos land in one place.

How do I collect photos from stag and doe guests?

Print a QR code at the door and game tables. Guests scan and upload photos and videos from the browser.

Do guests need an account?

No. They can upload without signing in; signed-in guests also get a private copy of their uploads.
