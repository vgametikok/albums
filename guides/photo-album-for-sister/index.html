A photo album for your sister: ideas she will love

Searches like “photo album for sister”, “photo album ideas for sister” and “gift for sister birthday” peak before birthdays and Christmas. A story album is a gift only you can make: your shared childhood, the fights, the trips and the private jokes, with your own voice telling the story. You can make it free in an evening.

When to give it

  • Her birthday – especially a milestone like 30, 40 or 50.
  • Christmas, when the family is together.
  • Her wedding or a new baby, as a look back at where it all started.
  • Moving away – to another province or abroad.

What to put in it

  • Childhood photos (scan or photograph the old prints).
  • Family road trips, the cottage, hockey or dance lessons, school concerts.
  • “Then and now” pairs: the same pose 20 years apart.
  • A short note under each photo about what was really going on.
  • Messages from parents, partners or kids recorded as voice notes.

Chapter ideas

  1. Growing up in the same house.
  2. Teenage years and the things Mom never found out about.
  3. Adventures together.
  4. Why you are my favourite person.

How to make it for free

On Albums you build a story album with chapters, photos, videos and voice notes, and you can narrate it in your own voice. Albums are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are saved at up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Create an album, for example “For Emma – 35 years of being sisters”.
  2. Add chapters, upload photos and short videos, write a line for each.
  3. Record a voice message in the browser.
  4. Invite one co-author – a brother or parent who is already your friend on Albums – to add their photos.
  5. Choose who can see it: Public, Friends or Only me, with per-person exceptions. Friends means all your friends, so to keep it a surprise, exclude your sister until the day and check the list again if you add new friends.
