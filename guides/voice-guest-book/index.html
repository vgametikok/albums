Voice guest book: collect voice messages from guests without renting a phone

An audio guest book lets guests leave a spoken message instead of a line in a notebook. Years later, hearing a grandparent's voice beats reading their handwriting. You don't need a rented vintage phone to do it.

What is a voice guest book?

A voice guest book (also called an audio guest book) collects recorded messages from guests at a wedding, birthday, anniversary or memorial. The classic version is a retro phone: guests pick up the receiver after the beep and talk. The recordings are delivered as audio files after the event.

How to set up a voice guest book with a QR code

  1. Create an Event Album and print its QR sign.
  2. Ask guests to record a short video message on their phone and upload it with their photos — no app, no sign-up.
  3. Add your own voice notes as the album owner: record the toasts' backstory, or grandparents telling how they met, and place them next to the photos.

Frequently asked questions

What is an audio guest book?

A guest book where people leave recorded voice messages instead of writing, usually via a retro phone or a QR code on their own phones.

Do I need to rent an audio guest book phone?

No. With a QR event album guests record video messages on their own phones and upload them with their photos, and you can add voice notes yourself.

Can remote guests leave a message?

Yes. Send them the album link and they can upload from anywhere.
