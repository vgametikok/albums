Baby's first Christmas: ideas and a keepsake album to look back on

“Baby's first Christmas” is searched by thousands of new parents in Australia every December, mostly for ornaments and outfits. Those are lovely, but the thing you'll treasure most is the story of the day. Here are ideas for a summer Christmas with a little one, and how to keep it all in a free album with videos and your own voice.

Ideas for baby's first Christmas

  • Santa photos: book a sensory-friendly session at the shopping centre to avoid long queues.
  • A first ornament: write the year and baby's name on it and hang it on the tree together.
  • Beach or backyard Christmas: a shady spot, a paddling pool and a hat for a hot day.
  • Christmas Eve box: new pyjamas, a book and something soft to cuddle.
  • A letter to baby: write what this year was like, to read together when they're older.
  • Family lunch: pass baby around the table and ask the grandparents to say a few words.

Turn the day into a story album

On Albums you create a free story album with chapters, photos, videos and voice notes. Free photos are kept up to 2048 px on the long edge. No ads, unlimited albums, and the album stays as long as you want it.

Frequently asked questions

What do you do for baby's first Christmas?

Many families do Santa photos, a first ornament, a Christmas Eve box and a letter to baby, plus a relaxed family lunch.

What should I keep from baby's first Christmas?

Photos and short videos of the day, who was there, the first presents and a few words about what baby was like that year.

Yes. A story album on Albums is free, with chapters, photos, videos and voice notes. No ads.

How do I share it only with family?
