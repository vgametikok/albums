Leaving do ideas: a send-off your colleague will remember

“Leaving do ideas for work”, “for colleagues” and “leaving party ideas for friends” are what people in the UK search when someone hands in their notice. Here are venue and activity ideas, tips for the speech and card, and a simple way to give the leaver every photo the team took on the night.

Pick a format that suits the leaver

  • Friday pub drinks: the classic leaving do, easy for everyone to drop by after work.
  • Team lunch: better for colleagues with childcare or long commutes.
  • Evening out: bowling, crazy golf, karaoke or a curry house for a livelier send-off.
  • In the office: cake, bubbly and a short speech at 4 pm when budgets are tight.
  • Hybrid team: a lunch in the office plus a video call for remote colleagues.

Leaving do games and activities

  • Office quiz: questions about the leaver's best moments and running jokes.
  • Memory jar: everyone writes a favourite memory or a piece of advice on a slip of paper.
  • Pub quiz table: book a quiz night and enter as one team in their honour.

The speech and the card

Keep the speech to two or three minutes: one funny story, one thing the team will miss, best wishes for what's next. Pass a card round the office early so nobody is left out, and collect for a gift a week before.

Collect everyone's photos with a QR code

Frequently asked questions

What do you do at a leaving do?

Usually drinks or a meal, a short speech, a card signed by the team and a gift. Some teams add a quiz or a memory jar.

Where can you have a leaving do for work?

A local pub, a restaurant, a bowling alley or karaoke bar, or simply the office with cake and drinks.

What can we give a colleague who is leaving?

A card signed by everyone, a gift from a group collection and an album with photos and messages from the team.

How do we collect photos from the leaving do?

Share a QR code. Everyone uploads photos and videos in the browser to one album, and you can review uploads first with “Hold guest photos”.
