End of year teacher gifts they’ll actually keep

With the school year in Australia wrapping up in December, parents search for “end of year teacher gifts from class”, “homemade” and “gifts they actually want”. Another mug or candle rarely makes the list. A story album with photos and thank-you messages from the whole class costs nothing and is easy for one class rep to put together.

Why a class album works

  • One gift from the whole class instead of 25 small ones.
  • Teachers often say the notes and drawings are what they keep.
  • It suits every budget: the album itself is free, so any class collection can still go to a voucher.

What to collect from families

  • A photo of each child with a drawing or a card saying “Thank you”.
  • Photos from excursions, sports carnival, book week and class events (only where parents are happy for them to be shared).
  • Short videos or voice messages: one sentence from each child about their favourite moment of the year.
  • A line from parents, if they like.

Chapter ideas

  1. Term 1: new faces.
  2. Excursions and special days.
  3. What we learned this year.
  4. Thank you from every one of us.

How to make it (free)

On Albums you build a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters, with optional voice narration. Albums are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored at up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google and create an album, e.g. “Thank you, Ms Nguyen – Year 2 2026”.
  2. Ask parents in the class group chat to send photos, short clips and voice messages to you.
  3. Add them chapter by chapter and write a caption for each child.
  4. One co-author can help, e.g. another class rep – they need to be on your friends list on Albums.
  5. Choose visibility: Public, Friends or Only me, with per-person exceptions. You can keep it on Only me while you build it, then switch to Public on the last day so the teacher can open the link without an account. An album on Only me cannot be opened by link.

Frequently asked questions

What do teachers actually want as an end of year gift?

Many teachers value personal notes, drawings and messages from the kids. A class album collects all of them in one place.

Is the story album free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited, and they stay as long as you want them.

Can another parent help build it?

Yes, one co-author per album, who must be on your friends list.

Does the teacher need an account?

Not if the album is set to Public – the link opens in any browser.
