Photo album for your wife: your story together as a gift

“Photo album for wife birthday” and “photo album gift for wife” are common searches in Canada, and most results are printed photo books. A printed book is lovely, but it can't hold the video of your first dance or your voice telling the story of how you met. An online story album can, and you can make one tonight for free.

What to put in a photo album for your wife

  • How it started: the first photo together, the first trip, the first apartment.
  • Big days: the proposal, the wedding, moving into your home, the kids arriving.
  • Seasons: cottage weekends, skating on the rink, fall road trips, summer by the lake.
  • Everyday life: Sunday breakfasts, the dog, her favourite coffee spot.
  • Things you never said out loud: a short caption under each photo is enough.
