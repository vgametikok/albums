First Christmas together gifts

In the UK people search “first Christmas together gifts”, “for him”, “for her” and “first Christmas together bauble”. A bauble goes on the tree; a story of your first months together is something you'll actually open again. It's free and you can make it in an evening.

Gift ideas that feel personal

  • A personalised bauble with the year – a classic for the tree.
  • A framed photo from your first date or first trip.
  • A “first Christmas together” box: their favourite snacks, a film for Christmas Eve and a handwritten card.
  • A story album of your relationship so far – easy to pair with any of the above.

What to put in the album

  • The first photo of you two together, however blurry.
  • Dates, weekends away, meeting the family, the first Sunday roast you cooked.
  • Short videos and voice notes: what you thought when you first met.

Chapter ideas

  1. How we met.
  2. Firsts.
  3. Our favourite places.
  4. This Christmas.
  5. Next year, together.

How to make it for free

On Albums you build a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters, and you can add voice narration. Albums are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored at up to 2048px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google and create an album, for example “Our first Christmas”.
  2. Add chapters, upload photos and videos and write a line under each.
  3. Record voice notes straight in the browser.
  4. You can invite one co-author, for example a friend who has the photos from the night you met; they have to be among your friends on Albums.
  5. Choose who sees it: Public, Friends or Only me. Friends means all your friends, so if your partner is on your friends list, use Friends except and exclude them until Christmas Day.
  6. On the day, change the visibility and send the link – it opens with a cover preview. A link to an album set to Only me won't open for anyone else.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get for your first Christmas together?

Something personal rather than expensive: a bauble with the year, a photo from your first date, or a story album of your first months.

What is a good first Christmas together gift for him?

A story album with your trips, his favourite moments and a voice note from you, plus a small gift like a bauble.

Can I keep it a surprise?

Yes. Keep it on Only me or exclude your partner with Friends except, then change the visibility on Christmas Day.

Is it free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited.
