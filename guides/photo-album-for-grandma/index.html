Photo album for grandma: ideas she'll actually look at

A photo album for grandma is the gift that gets opened again in January — and every time the grandkids visit. Printed books take weeks, and most of your best photos are already on your phone. Here are photo album ideas for grandma — for a birthday, Christmas or Mother's Day — and how to turn them into an online album with the kids' voices that she can open from a link.

What to put in it

  • Her with each grandchild — baby photos next to recent ones.
  • Family holidays: Thanksgiving, Christmas at the cottage, summer at the lake.
  • Recipes and handwritten notes photographed from her kitchen.
  • Old prints you scanned: her wedding, your parents as kids.
  • Videos: a short clip of the kids saying “hi Nana” means more than any caption.

Ways to structure the album

  • One chapter per grandchild.
  • One chapter per year of family life.
  • “Things we learned from you”: one photo, one memory.
  • Seasons: spring garden, summer cottage, fall colours, winter holidays.

Add the kids' voices

A short voice note on the best photos — a story, a song, “I love you Nana” — turns a slideshow into a gift. She can play the album as a slideshow with narration.

What should I put in a photo album for grandma?

Photos of her with each grandchild, family holidays, scanned old prints, recipes and short videos or voice notes from the kids.

How do I make an online photo album for grandma?

Create a story album, add chapters, upload photos and videos, record voice notes and share the link privately with her.
