A Christmas gift for your mother-in-law that she can't buy herself

Type “christmas gift for mother in law” and suggestions like “who has everything” and “you don't like” pop up – it's one of the trickier names on the list. If she already has the candles, the scarves and the mugs, a story album of the year with her family can be the gift she actually keeps opening.

Why a story album works

  • Most mothers-in-law care more about the grandkids and family moments than about another gadget.
  • The photos already exist – they're just spread across everyone's phones and group chats.
  • It's free, so you can pair it with a small physical gift: her favourite chocolates, a plant, a nice tea.

What to put in it

  • The year with you: summer at the cottage, Thanksgiving dinner, a hockey game, the first snow.
  • The grandkids: school photos, short videos, a “Merry Christmas, Grandma” voice message.
  • A few older family photos, if your partner can find them at home.
  • A short voice note from your partner – and one from you.

Chapter ideas

  1. Our year together.
  2. The grandkids.
  3. Family dinners.
  4. Thank you, Mom.

How to make it (free)

On Albums you create a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters and narrated with your own voice. Albums and posts are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google or Telegram and create an album, e.g. “Christmas 2026 – for Linda”.
  2. Ask your partner and siblings-in-law to send photos and voice messages.
  3. Add them chapter by chapter and write one line under each.
  4. New albums are Public by default. To keep the surprise, set it to Only me while you work on it. If a co-author is helping, do not leave the album on Only me — they would lose access. Use Friends or Friends, except… while you work together, then switch to Public to send the link.
  5. You can add one co-author – for example your partner, once you're friends on Albums.

How to give it

On Christmas morning, open it together on the TV or a tablet. If you'd like her to open it on her own, switch it to Public and send the link: Public albums open in the browser without signing in, with a cover preview. Friends albums are visible only to your signed-in friends, and Only me albums don't open by link. You can also download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a mother-in-law who has everything?

Something personal usually lands best: a story album with the year's family photos, the grandkids' videos and voice messages.

Is the album free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited.

Does she need an account to see it?

Not if the album is Public: the link opens in the browser. Friends albums require her to sign in and be your friend on Albums.

Can I download the album?

Yes, the whole album can be downloaded as one ZIP file.
