Friendsgiving ideas: potluck themes, games and one album for every guest's photos

Friendsgiving is Thanksgiving with the friends who feel like family — a potluck, mismatched chairs and a long table that somehow fits twelve. Everyone takes photos of the turkey, the pie and the chaos, but by Monday the pictures are scattered across group chats. Here are Friendsgiving ideas that work in a city apartment or a backyard, plus a simple way to collect every photo in one album.

Potluck themes that actually work

  • Classic Thanksgiving, shared: assign turkey, sides and dessert so nobody doubles up on mashed potatoes.
  • Regional potluck: Southern sides, New England pies, West Coast salads — guests bring a dish from home.
  • Friendsgiving brunch: earlier start, mimosas, less oven stress.
  • Leftovers night: for the crew that already did family Thanksgiving — sandwiches, soup and pie.

Games and moments guests remember

  • Gratitude round: one thing each person is thankful for this year — short, no speeches.
  • Friendsgiving bingo: “brought a store-bought pie”, “fought over the drumstick”.
  • Then-and-now: photos from your first Friendsgiving next to this year's table.
  • Photo missions: with the host, with the turkey, with the whole crew — only counts if it goes in the album.

Collect every guest's photos with one QR code

An event album on Albums replaces “send me your pics” the next day:

  1. Create the album, e.g. “Friendsgiving 2026”.
  2. Print the QR code on the invite, place cards and near the dessert table.
  3. Guests scan it with their phone camera and upload photos and videos from the browser — nothing to install.
  4. Turn on “Hold guest photos” to approve uploads before anyone else sees them.
  5. After the weekend, download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Guests can upload without signing in — their photos then appear as anonymous. Anyone who signs in also gets their own private copy with just the photos they uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

What is Friendsgiving?

Friendsgiving is a Thanksgiving-style meal celebrated with friends — usually a potluck dinner before or instead of family Thanksgiving.

What are good Friendsgiving ideas?

A themed potluck, a short gratitude round, Friendsgiving bingo, then-and-now photos and a QR code album for every guest's pictures.

How do I collect photos from Friendsgiving guests?

Put a QR code on the invite and the table. Guests scan it and upload photos and videos from the browser into one album.

Do guests need an account?

No. They can upload without signing in; signed-in guests also get a private copy of their own uploads.
