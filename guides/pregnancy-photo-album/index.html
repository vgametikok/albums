Pregnancy photo album: the nine months, told in chapters

A pregnancy photo album is more than a folder of bump pictures. In the US, parents often want one place for the positive test, monthly photos against the same wall, anatomy-scan printouts and the voice of the people waiting with them. Here is a simple way to build that story online — privately, with chapters you can keep filling until the first days at home.

What to put in a pregnancy photo album

  • The beginning: the test, who you told first, the first appointment card.
  • Month by month: one bump photo in the same spot, plus a sentence about how that month felt.
  • Scan pictures: the heartbeat visit and the anatomy scan, dated, with a short caption — not a pile of unmarked files.
  • The in-between: nursery painting, the hospital bag, the craving that made no sense at 2 a.m.
  • People around you: a short voice note from a partner, a sibling or a grandparent, spoken as if the baby could already hear it.
