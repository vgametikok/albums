A Christmas gift for your father-in-law who has everything

Search suggestions for “christmas gift for father in law” include “who has everything”, “small” and “reddit” – a sign that many people are stuck. If he already owns the gadgets and the golf balls, give him something no store sells: a story album about the family, with photos, short videos and voices of the grandkids. It is free to make.

Why a story album works for him

  • He has the tools, the sweaters and the whiskey. He does not have a year of family moments in one place.
  • It is personal without being sentimental overload: short captions, real photos.
  • It costs nothing, so it pairs well with a small gift like his favorite coffee or a sports ticket.

What to put in it

  • Photos of the grandkids from this year: first day of school, ball games, Halloween, Thanksgiving.
  • Pictures of him with your spouse, old and new, if you can get them from the family.
  • Short videos: the kids saying “Merry Christmas, Grandpa”.
  • A voice note from you and your spouse saying thank you.

Chapter ideas

  1. Our year in photos.
  2. The grandkids.
  3. Trips, games and the backyard.
  4. Thank you, Dad.

How to make it (free)

On Albums you build a story album from photos, videos and voice notes, split into chapters, with optional voice narration. Albums are unlimited and stay as long as you want them. Free photos are stored at up to 2048 px on the long edge.

  1. Sign in with Google or Telegram and create an album, e.g. “Merry Christmas, Dad – 2026”.
  2. Add photos and clips chapter by chapter and write a short caption for each.
  3. Record a voice note right in the browser.
  4. Invite one co-author – for example your partner – who must be on your friends list on Albums.
  5. Choose visibility: Public, Friends or Only me. New albums are public, so switch to Only me while you build it if you want to keep the surprise. Friends means all your friends, so if he is on your friends list, use Friends, except… and leave him out until Christmas. If a co-author is helping, do not leave the album on Only me — they would lose access. Use Friends, except… and exclude him until Christmas.

Giving it on Christmas

Open it together on the TV or a tablet after dinner, or change the visibility and text him the link. A Public album opens in any browser with a cover preview, nothing to install. A Friends album opens only for your signed-in friends. An album on Only me cannot be opened by link. He can also download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a father-in-law who has everything?

Something personal he cannot buy, like a story album with photos and voice messages from the family.

Is it free?

Yes. Story albums are free and unlimited, and they stay as long as you want them.

Does he need an account to see it?

Not if the album is Public – the link opens in any browser. For Friends, he needs to sign in and be on your friends list.

Can he keep a copy?

Yes, the whole album can be downloaded as one ZIP file.
