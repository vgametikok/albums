60th birthday party ideas

In Canada people search “60th birthday party ideas”, “for mom”, “for dad at home” and “for husband”. Here’s a simple plan: format, theme, food, a few moments to remember, and an easy way to collect every guest’s photos and videos.

Pick the format

  • Party at home: an open house in the afternoon so family and neighbours can drop by.
  • Dinner out: a private room at a favourite restaurant for close family.
  • Cottage weekend if the birthday falls in summer.
  • Surprise party with friends from every stage of their life.

Theme ideas

  • Born in the 60s: music and decor from the year they were born.
  • Sixty and fabulous: gold, black and white, a toast and a cake.
  • Their passion: hockey, golf, gardening or travel.

Food

  • A buffet or potluck with a few dishes they love.
  • A charcuterie board and a dessert table.
  • A signature drink, plus non-alcoholic options.

Moments to plan

  • A memory table with old photos from each decade.
  • Short speeches from a child, a sibling and a friend.
  • “60 reasons we love you” written on cards by the guests.

Collect everyone’s photos with a QR code

Every guest takes photos on their phone. With an event album on Albums they all end up in one place:

  1. Create an album, e.g. “Dad’s 60th”.
  2. Put the QR code on the tables, the memory table or the invitation.
  3. Guests scan it with their camera and upload photos and videos in the browser, with nothing to install.
  4. Turn on Hold guest photos to review uploads before they appear.
  5. Afterwards, download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Guests who upload without signing in appear as anonymous. Signed-in guests also get a private draft copy of only their own uploads.

Frequently asked questions

How do you celebrate a 60th birthday at home?

An afternoon open house with a buffet, a memory table with old photos, a few short speeches and a cake.

What is a good theme for a 60th?

The decade they were born in, a gold-and-black “sixty” party, or their favourite hobby.

How do I collect photos from all the guests?

Use a QR code: everyone uploads photos and videos to one album from the browser, and you can approve them first.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. The QR code opens the album in the phone’s browser.
