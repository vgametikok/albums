Bachelorette party photo scavenger hunt (and one private album for the photos)

A bachelorette weekend produces hundreds of photos across a dozen phones — and not all of them are meant for the wedding slideshow. A private QR album collects everything in one place and lets you decide what goes where.

Why the group chat isn't enough

The group chat is great for planning, terrible for photos. It compresses everything, buries the best photos under logistics, and leaves out the bride's sister who is not in that chat. After the trip, someone always asks “can everyone send me their photos?” and gets three replies.

A shared album with one QR code or link solves it. Everyone uploads from the phone browser, with no app and no account. The maid of honour or organizer owns the album and decides who sees what.

Setting it up

  1. Create the album with a fun name: “Lisbon Last Fling” or “Jess's Bach Weekend”.
  2. Keep it hidden so only people with the link can open it.
  3. Turn on approval if you want every upload to pass the organizer before others see it.
  4. Share the link in the group chat before the trip, and print a small QR card for the rental house or the dinner table.

Bachelorette party photo album for multi-day trips

Bachelorette weekends often span several days and cities. Use chapters for each day or activity — “Friday dinner”, “Boat day”, “Spa”, “Night out” — so the album reads as a story instead of a jumble. Everyone can upload whenever they get Wi-Fi; the link never expires during the trip and the upload window never closes.

Bachelorette party photo scavenger hunt list

  • The whole group in matching outfits, from several angles
  • The bride-to-be's reaction to each surprise
  • Getting ready together
  • The view from wherever you are staying
  • One photo from each person of their favourite moment

Short videos are welcome too — clips up to 50 MB per file, about a minute from a phone, upload directly.

Keeping it discreet

What happens on the trip doesn't have to land in the wedding album. With Albums every photo can be shown to everyone, kept for close friends only, or hidden completely. Approval mode means nothing appears before the organizer sees it. Guests can upload anonymously, so nobody's name is attached unless they choose. If the link travels further than intended, issue a new one: the old code stops working and the group keeps access.

The live wall at the party

If you are celebrating at home or a rented space, put the live photo wall on a TV. Watching photos from the day pop up in real time is entertaining on its own, and it gets the quieter friends uploading too.

Bachelor parties work the same way

Golf trips, camping weekends, city breaks — the same setup works for a bachelor or stag party. The best man creates the album, keeps it hidden and approves uploads. Chapters per activity keep it tidy.

After the trip

  • Curate a highlight set. Show the best photos to the whole group and keep the rest for close friends.
  • Make a gift. Add a cover and short voice notes from each friend; the album becomes a pre-wedding present for the bride or groom.
  • Feed the wedding. Pick a few tasteful photos for the rehearsal dinner slideshow or the wedding album.
