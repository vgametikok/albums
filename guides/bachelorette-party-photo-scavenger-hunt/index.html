Bachelorette party photo scavenger hunt (and one private album for the photos)

A bachelorette party photo scavenger hunt turns the weekend into a game and leaves the bride with a hilarious album of memories. Below you'll find ready-to-use scavenger hunt lists (classic, clean and city-trip versions), rules that keep it fun, more bachelorette party photo ideas, and a simple way to gather every photo from the group into one private album.

How a bachelorette photo scavenger hunt works

  1. Split the group into teams of two to four (or let the bride compete against everyone).
  2. Give each team the same list of photo challenges, on paper or as a message.
  3. Set a time limit — an hour in the afternoon or the whole night out.
  4. Teams complete challenges and upload proof photos and videos to a shared album.
  5. Points are tallied at the end; the winning team gets a prize, and the bride gets the album.

A shared album with a QR code makes step 4 easy: everyone uploads from the phone browser, no app or account needed, and the maid of honour can check entries in real time.

  1. Selfie with a stranger wearing a veil
  2. Photo with someone named the same as the groom
  3. The bride getting a free drink
  4. A stranger giving marriage advice (video, 2 points)
  5. The whole group recreating a famous album cover
  6. Someone proposing to the bride (3 points)
  7. Photo with a bartender wearing the bride's sash
  8. A group pyramid
  9. Matching outfits with a stranger
  10. The bride dancing on a stage
  11. A photo with a married couple celebrating an anniversary
  12. A photo of the bride with something blue

Clean and family-friendly version

  1. The group jumping in the air at the same moment
  2. Recreate the couple's first date
  3. The bride with a dog
  4. A stranger signing the bride's sash
  5. Everyone making a heart with their hands
  6. A photo in front of a church or a wedding dress shop
  7. Find something old, new, borrowed and blue
  8. The bride trying on a hat in a shop
  9. A photo with the oldest couple you can find
  10. The group in a photo booth

City-trip or destination version

  1. The group at the city's most famous landmark
  2. Local food the bride has never tried
  3. A street musician playing a song for the bride
  4. A photo with a local in traditional dress
  5. The best sunset photo
  6. The bride on a boat, bike or scooter

Rules that keep it fun

  • Photos and videos must go to the album to count — no “trust me”.
  • Every team member must appear in at least half of the photos.
  • Be kind to strangers. If someone says no, move on.
  • Time limit is strict; late uploads don't count.
  • The maid of honour judges, and her decision is final.

Prize ideas

  • Free drinks for the winning team
  • A small trophy, crown or sash for the winners
  • The losing team plans next morning's brunch
  • Custom photo prints from the album

Bachelorette party photo album for multi-day trips

Destination weekends generate hundreds of photos across several phones. Set up chapters for each day or activity — “Arrival”, “Spa day”, “Night out”, “Brunch”. Share the link in the group chat before the trip so photos from the airport count too. After the trip, the album becomes a single place everyone can browse instead of five different camera rolls.

Keeping it discreet

Bachelorette photos aren't always for everyone. Keep the album hidden so only people with the link can open it. Each photo can be shown to everyone, kept for friends, or hidden. Before you share any of it with the couple or family, hide what should stay within the group.

The album on a TV at the party

If you're celebrating at a rented house or a private room, open the album on a TV and refresh it from time to time. Watching scavenger hunt entries appear keeps the energy up and lets the judges score live.

Bachelor parties work the same way

Swap the list for golf, sports bars, a cabin weekend or a city crawl. Everything else — teams, points, a shared album — works the same. For general event tips, see how to collect photos from event guests.

After the trip

  • Sort photos and hide anything private.
  • Pick the best for a gift album or a slideshow at the rehearsal dinner.
  • Download everything as a ZIP with captions.
  • As the album owner, add voice notes to the funniest photos with the story behind them.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put on a bachelorette party photo scavenger hunt?

Challenges like a selfie with a stranger in a veil, a stranger's marriage advice on video, the bride getting a free drink, a group pyramid and something old, new, borrowed and blue.

How long should a bachelorette scavenger hunt last?

Usually one to two hours, or the whole night out with a hard deadline for uploads.

How do teams submit their photos?

Upload them to a shared album from the phone browser via a link or QR code; no app or account needed.

Is there a clean version of the scavenger hunt?

Yes — use family-friendly prompts like recreating the couple's first date, finding something blue, or a photo with the oldest couple you can find.

Can we keep the bachelorette photos private?

Yes. Keep the album hidden, and show each photo to everyone, keep it for friends, or hide it.
