Family Christmas party ideas

In the US people search “family christmas party ideas”, “for large groups” and “at home”. Here is a simple plan for a party with cousins, grandparents and kids – and a way to get everyone's photos in one place afterwards.

Food that works for a crowd

  • A potluck: each household brings one dish, you cover the ham or turkey.
  • A hot cocoa bar with marshmallows and candy canes.
  • A cookie decorating table for the kids.

Games for all ages

  • White elephant gift exchange with a set price limit.
  • Christmas trivia, teams by family branch.
  • Matching pajamas or a family ugly sweater contest.
  • A carol sing-along before dessert.

Ideas for large groups

  • Rent a church hall or community room if the house is too small.
  • Print name tags with “whose kid is whose” for the in-laws.
  • Take one big family group photo early, before anyone leaves.

Every guest's photos with one QR code

Everyone takes photos on their phone, and they end up in a dozen group chats. With an event album on Albums they land in one place:

  1. Create the album, e.g. “Johnson Family Christmas 2026”.
  2. Put the QR code on the fridge, the tables or in the invite.
  3. Guests scan it with the phone camera and upload photos and videos in the browser – nothing to install.
  4. Turn on Hold guest photos to approve uploads before they appear.
  5. Afterwards download the whole album as one ZIP file.

Guests who upload without signing in appear anonymously. Signed-in guests also get a private draft copy of only their own uploads.

Frequently asked questions

How do you host a family Christmas party for a large group?

Make it a potluck, rent a hall if needed, and plan a few games like a white elephant exchange and Christmas trivia.

What games work for all ages?

White elephant, Christmas trivia by family teams, an ugly sweater contest and a carol sing-along.

How do I collect everyone's photos?

Put one QR code on the tables. Guests upload photos and videos in the browser to the same album, and you can approve them first.

Do guests need to install anything?

No. They scan the code with the phone camera and upload in the browser.
