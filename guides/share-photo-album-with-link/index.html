How to Share a Photo Album With a Link: Free, Private and Easy to Open

Sending 80 photos one by one in a chat is slow, compresses them and buries them under messages. Sharing a photo album with a link is cleaner: one message, and everyone sees the whole album. But links come with questions. Who can open it? Will it show a nice preview? Can people download the photos? Can you stop sharing later? This guide answers those questions for the most common services and shows how link sharing works on Albums.ink.

Why share an album by link

  • One message instead of dozens of attachments.
  • Better quality than photos sent through most chat apps.
  • Order and context: photos stay in the sequence and chapters you chose.
  • Easy updates: add photos later and the same link shows them.

Sharing a Google Photos album with a link

  1. Open the album in Google Photos and tap Share.
  2. Choose Create link.
  3. Paste the link into a chat or email.

Anyone with the link can usually view the album, and you can turn link sharing off in the album's sharing options.

Sharing an iCloud or Apple Photos album with a link

On iPhone, a shared album can be made into a public website in the album's people settings. Anyone with that link can view the photos in a browser, which is handy for Android friends. You can turn the public website off again.

Sharing an album with a link on Albums.ink

Every album on Albums.ink has a share link. When you paste it into a chat or social app, it shows a cover preview with the album title and cover photo, so it looks inviting instead of like a random URL.

  1. Sign in with Google and create an album.
  2. Upload photos and videos and pick a cover.
  3. Choose privacy: public, friends only or private.
  4. Copy the link and send it.

Who can open the link?

This is the most important question, and it depends on privacy settings:

  • Public album: anyone with the link can view it, no sign-in needed. To like, comment or save it, people sign in with Google.
  • Friends-only album: only your friends on Albums.ink, plus any people you added as exceptions.
  • Private album: only you and the specific people you add as exceptions.
  • Per-file visibility: inside any album, individual photos or videos can be hidden from viewers.

So you can send the same link to a big group chat and still be sure only the right people see the album.

Making a link look good in chats

  • Choose a strong cover photo: a face or a wide view works better than a detail.
  • Use a clear title: "Lisbon, May 2026" beats "IMG folder 3".
  • Add a one-line message when you send it: what the album is and why it matters.

Letting people add photos too

Link sharing is about viewing. If you want someone else to add photos, invite them as a co-author. On the free plan each album can have one co-author chosen from your friends; Pro allows up to 10.

Can people download photos from a shared album?

As the owner, you can download a whole album at any time. Assume that anything a viewer can see on a screen can be saved with a screenshot, on any service. Keep sensitive photos private or hidden with per-file visibility.

How to stop sharing

On most services you can disable the link or change the album's privacy. On Albums.ink, switch the album to private or friends only, or remove a person's exception. Hidden photos stay hidden even for people who can open the album.

Link sharing vs. sending photos in a chat

  • Chats are instant, but photos get compressed, mixed with messages and lost when phones change.
  • Albums keep photos organized, in better quality, with captions, chapters and voice notes.
  • A link takes seconds to send and works for people on iPhone, Android and computers.

Typical uses

  • A wedding or birthday album for guests who were there.
  • A travel album for friends and family who were not.
  • A monthly family album for grandparents.
  • A portfolio or project album for clients or colleagues.
  • A school or club album for parents, kept friends only.

Photo quality on Albums.ink

Free photos are saved at up to 2048 px on the long side, enough for a great view on any screen. Pro ($9.99/month) keeps photos up to 4K, allows videos up to 500 MB and adds stats so you can see how your albums are viewed.

Safety checklist before you send a link

  1. Check the album privacy.
  2. Hide any photo you are not sure about.
  3. Remove location-revealing photos of your home if the album is public.
  4. Think about kids: prefer private or friends-only albums.
  5. Send the link to the right chat.

Limits to know

Albums.ink is a website, not an app, and does not sync your phone automatically. Upload the photos you want, and share from any browser.

Writing the message that goes with the link

A link with no context often goes unopened. Add one or two sentences: "Here are the photos from Saturday, there is a voice note from Grandma in chapter 2!" Mention a detail that makes people curious. For big groups, pin the message so latecomers find it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I share a photo album with a link?

Create an album, choose its privacy, copy the share link and send it. On Albums.ink the link shows a cover preview in chats.

Can anyone with the link see my album?

Only if it is public. Friends-only and private albums on Albums.ink are visible only to friends or to the people you add as exceptions.

Can I share an album with Android and iPhone users?

Yes. Albums.ink works in any browser, so the link opens on iPhone, Android and computers.

Can I stop sharing later?

Yes. Change the album to private, remove exceptions or hide individual photos.

Is sharing albums by link free?

Yes. Unlimited albums and link sharing are free on Albums.ink.
