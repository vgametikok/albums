Photo album for mum: a gift she can open on her phone

A photo album for mum beats another candle — especially when the kids live in different cities and the best photos are stuck in separate camera rolls. In Australia, “mum” is the word on the gift tag. Here is a simple way to build a story album with chapters, short voice notes and a link she can keep.

What to put in a photo album for mum

  • Childhood to now: scan a few old prints, then jump to recent holidays and Sunday lunches.
  • One chapter per kid: each sibling adds photos and a 20-second voice note.
  • Places she loves: the beach house, the garden, the café she always chooses.
  • Everyday rituals: morning coffee, walking the dog — the small stuff she will replay.
