Digital guest book: how to collect wishes, photos and memories with a QR code

A paper guest book collects a few hurried signatures. A digital guest book collects much more: photos, video wishes and messages from people who couldn't come — all in one place you can keep. This guide explains what a digital guest book is, how a QR code guest book works at weddings, funerals and parties, what free options exist, and how to get guests to actually leave something.

What is a digital guest book?

A digital guest book is an online page where guests leave messages and memories instead of writing in a paper book. Guests usually reach it by scanning a QR code at the event or opening a link. Depending on the service, they can leave text, photos, videos or voice recordings.

Paper vs digital guest book

  • Paper: beautiful on the table, but guests write one line in a queue, and nobody who missed the event can sign.
  • Digital: no queue, guests can add photos and videos, remote friends can join, and nothing gets lost or damaged.
  • Both: many hosts keep a small paper book for nostalgia and put a QR code next to it for photos and longer messages.

How a QR code guest book works

  1. You create an online album for the event and get a link and a printable QR code.
  2. You place the QR sign next to the entrance, the gift table or the paper guest book.
  3. Guests scan it with the phone camera; a page opens in the browser — no app, no account.
  4. They upload photos and short video wishes; guests who sign in can also leave a written comment.
  5. Everything appears in one album that you can organize, hide parts of and download.

On Albums, the album owner can also add voice notes next to photos, so the guest book turns into a story told in your own words.

Digital guest book for a wedding

At weddings a digital guest book does two jobs at once: it collects wishes and the photos guests take all evening. Ideas:

  • Put the QR sign next to the paper book and on every table.
  • Ask for a photo plus one sentence of advice for the couple.
  • Show the QR code on the venue screen during the reception.
  • Send the link with the thank-you message so guests can add photos later.

Digital guest book for a funeral or memorial

At a funeral, a digital guest book lets people share memories and photos of the person who died — including friends and colleagues the family has never met. It also helps those who couldn't travel. Keep it gentle:

  • Use a calm wording: “If you have a photo or a memory of Margaret, please share it here.”
  • Keep the album hidden so only people with the link can open it.
  • Hide any entry the family doesn’t want visible. You can also turn on “Hold guest photos”: new uploads stay hidden until you let them through.
  • Share the link with the funeral notice, not only at the service.

Digital guest book for birthdays, anniversaries and other events

The same approach works for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, retirements, baby showers and company events. Ask guests for a photo with the host and a short wish; at the end you have a keepsake that is far richer than a card.

For family events, a digital guest book is also a gift for later: in ten years, a grandchild can scroll through photos and wishes from everyone who came, instead of a page of signatures nobody remembers. That is why many hosts keep the album as a family archive and add new chapters for the next celebrations.

Is there a free digital guest book?

Yes, a few routes cost nothing:

  • A shared document or form — guests type wishes, but there are no photos in one place and it looks impersonal.
  • A shared cloud folder — free, but guests often need an account, and wishes and photos are separated.
  • Free tiers of guest book apps — often limited in uploads, quality or time.

A paid event album makes sense when you want photos, videos and wishes together, no accounts for guests and control over what appears.

What to write on the guest book sign

  • “Please sign our guest book! Scan and add a photo or a short video wish — no app needed.”
  • “Leave us a memory: a photo, a video or a few words.”
  • “Can't find a pen? Scan to sign our digital guest book.”
  • For memorials: “Share a photo or a memory of [name]. Thank you for being here.”

Sign placement tips are collected in wedding QR code table signs.

Prompts that help guests write more

  • Your favourite memory with the hosts
  • Advice for the next ten years
  • A wish for the couple or the birthday person
  • How you met
  • A song that reminds you of them

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital guest book?

An online page where guests leave wishes, photos, videos and memories, usually reached by scanning a QR code at the event.

How does a QR code guest book work?

Guests scan the code with their phone camera, an upload page opens in the browser, and they add photos and videos, such as a short video wish — no app or account.

Is there a free digital guest book?

Shared documents, forms or cloud folders are free, but guests may need accounts and photos and wishes end up in different places.

Can I use a digital guest book at a funeral?

Yes. Keep the album hidden, hide anything the family doesn’t want visible, and share the link with the funeral notice so distant friends can add memories.

Can guests who didn't attend sign it?

Yes. Send them the link and they can add photos and wishes from anywhere.
