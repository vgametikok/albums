Winter wedding ideas for a cosy UK celebration

Next to dresses and outfits, “winter wedding ideas”, “colors” and “venues” show up when you start typing “winter wedding”. Winter dates are often easier to book, and the season gives you candlelight, velvet and mulled wine. Here are practical ideas for a UK winter wedding – and how to get every guest’s photos afterwards.

Plan around the light

  • In December the sun sets around 4pm in much of the UK, so many couples move the ceremony earlier.
  • Schedule couple portraits soon after the ceremony while there is still daylight.
  • Plan for rain and wind: umbrellas at the door, an indoor drinks reception.

Cosy touches

  • Candles, fairy lights and greenery: eucalyptus, holly, pine.
  • Blankets or wraps on chairs for any outdoor moment.
  • Mulled wine, hot chocolate or a mince pie station during the drinks reception.
  • Deep colours: burgundy, forest green, navy, gold.

Food and entertainment

  • A hearty wedding breakfast: roasts, pies, sticky toffee pudding.
  • A ceilidh or a band to keep everyone warm on the dance floor.
  • A sparkler exit if your venue allows it.

Many couples start earlier, around midday or early afternoon, to get daylight for photos before sunset.
