Bridal shower ideas: themes, games and every guest's photos in one album

Searches like “bridal shower ideas at home”, “on a budget” and “for small group” show what most hosts in the US are juggling: a party that feels special without a venue bill. Here are themes, games and planning tips — and an easy way to end up with every photo guests took, not just the ones on your own phone.

Who hosts and when

The maid of honor and bridesmaids often host, sometimes together with family. Most showers happen a few weeks to a couple of months before the wedding. Ask the bride who she wants on the guest list before you send invites.

Bridal shower themes

  • Brunch and bubbly: a mimosa bar, pastries and a fruit board.
  • Garden tea party: florals, tea sandwiches and mismatched china.
  • Something blue: a color palette that ties into the wedding tradition.
  • Taco or pasta night: a relaxed dinner instead of a formal lunch.
  • Destination theme: decor and snacks inspired by the honeymoon spot.

Bridal shower games

  • Bridal bingo: guests fill cards with gifts they expect her to open.
  • How well do you know the bride? A quiz with answers recorded beforehand.
  • Advice and date-night cards: guests write tips or ideas for the couple.
  • Guess the dress: teams sketch what they think she'll wear.

At home and on a budget

A living room or backyard works for a small group. Go potluck or brunch, borrow chairs and glassware, and make one photo corner with flowers and a sign instead of decorating the whole house.

Frequently asked questions

Who usually hosts a bridal shower?

Often the maid of honor and bridesmaids, sometimes with the bride's or groom's family.

How do I host a bridal shower at home on a budget?

Keep the guest list small, go potluck or brunch, borrow tableware and decorate one photo corner instead of the whole house.

What games are good for a small group?

Bridal bingo, a “how well do you know the bride” quiz and advice cards work well with 8–15 guests.

How can I collect all the photos guests take?

Put a QR code on the table. Guests upload photos and videos in the browser to one shared album, and you can review uploads first with “Hold guest photos”.
