Photo album for your girlfriend: a birthday or anniversary gift with your story in it

Most searches for a “photo album for girlfriend” in the UK end at a printed photo book or a mini album. They are lovely, but they cannot hold the video from your first trip to Edinburgh or your voice explaining why that rainy day in Brighton still makes you laugh. An online story album can. Here is how to make one she will want to open again.

What to put in a photo album for your girlfriend

  • The beginning: the first photo of the two of you, a copy of the first message, the place you first met.
  • Trips and weekends: a city break, a cottage in the Lakes, the pub quiz you nearly won.
  • Little things: her coffee order, the film you always rewatch, the dog you both stop for.
  • Her people: friends and family who have become yours too.
  • What comes next: a last chapter with plans — a trip, a gig, a new flat.
  • One caption per photo: one line is enough. Inside jokes count.
