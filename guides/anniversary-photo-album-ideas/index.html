Anniversary photo album ideas: how to tell your story together

An anniversary photo album works best when it tells a story, not just shows pictures. Whether it is your first anniversary or your parents' 40th, here are ideas for structuring the album, captions that are easy to write, and how to make it online so you can share it with family from Vancouver to Halifax — or keep it just between the two of you.

Pick a structure

  • Year by year: one chapter per year together, from the first date to today.
  • Firsts: first date, first trip, first apartment, first pet, first winter at the cottage.
  • Places: every city, campsite or lake you have been to together.
  • Then and now: the same spot or pose, years apart.

Ideas for a first anniversary

Keep it personal: how you met, the first photo of the two of you, inside jokes and a page of “things I love about you.” A voice note reading a short letter will mean more than any caption.

Ideas for milestone anniversaries (10, 25, 40, 50)

  • Ask children, grandchildren and friends for one photo and one memory each.
  • Add the wedding photos and the story of the day as the opening chapter.
  • Record the couple telling how they met — their voices are what the family will come back to.
  • Play the album as a slideshow at the anniversary dinner.

Caption ideas that are easy to write

  • “The day we…” — one sentence per photo is enough.
  • “What I remember most about this day is…”
  • “Where we were living / what we were driving / what song was always on.”
  • “If I could go back to this moment, I would tell you…”

Frequently asked questions

What do you put in an anniversary photo album?

Photos from your time together arranged by year, place or “firsts”, short captions, and if possible voice messages or a recorded letter.

How do I make an anniversary photo album online?

Create a story album, add chapters, upload photos and videos, record voice notes and share it by link or keep it private.

Is it free?

Yes. Story albums on Albums are free with unlimited albums and no ads. Free photo uploads are up to 30 MB per file.

Can my partner add photos too?

Yes. You can invite one friend per album as co-author, so you can both add photos and stories.
