Anniversary party photo sharing: collect guest photos and decades of memories

A 25th, 40th or 50th anniversary celebrates a whole life together. With one QR code, guests can share photos from tonight and from the decades before — and the couple ends up with a story nobody could have assembled alone.

Why anniversaries need a shared album

Anniversary parties are usually organized by children or close friends, and they often come with a memory slideshow. Collecting the photos for that slideshow means chasing relatives for weeks. Meanwhile, the photos taken at the party itself end up scattered across guests' phones.

A shared album with one QR code handles both. Guests open the link or scan the code and upload from the browser. No app, no account — important when many guests are the couple's friends of the same generation.

Before the party: collect the past

Send the album link with the invitation and ask for old photos: the wedding day, the first apartment, family holidays, the children growing up. Friends often have photos the family has never seen. Ask contributors to add a caption with the year and place.

Organize with chapters by decade — “The 1970s”, “The 1980s” and so on — or by milestone: “The wedding”, “The kids”, “Travels”, “Tonight”. By the day of the party you have a ready-made slideshow.

At the party

  • Run the live wall. Put the album on a TV or projector. Old photos uploaded in advance and new photos from the evening appear together. It becomes the conversation piece of the night.
  • QR signs on each table with a line like “Celebrating 50 years of Anna & Peter — add your photos, old or new. No app needed.”
  • Recreate the wedding photo. Pose the couple as in their original wedding portrait and upload both versions.
  • Ask for toasts on video. Short clips of speeches and the first dance upload directly; up to 50 MB per file, about a minute from a phone.
  • Help older guests. Ask grandchildren to sit with the older guests for a minute and upload their photos for them.

Voices, not just photos

The album owner can add voice notes and text next to photos. Record the couple telling how they met beside their first photo together, or a sibling sharing a story from the wedding. Years later these recordings are often treasured more than any single picture.

Guests who can't travel

Old friends and relatives abroad can still take part. Send them the link so they can follow the live wall and add their photos and messages. The upload window never closes, so late contributions are always welcome.

Privacy

Keep the album hidden so only guests with the link can see it, and turn on approval if you'd like to check uploads first. Guests can upload anonymously. If the link travels too far, issue a new one: the old code stops working and those already in the album keep access.

The keepsake

After the party, sort the album: show the best to everyone, keep some for the family, hide duplicates. Add a cover photo of the couple and you have a gift that tells their story in their friends' and family's own photos and voices.
