Memory book ideas: what to include for friends, couples, parents and kids

A memory book gathers photos, words and small stories about a person or a stretch of life — a best friend's 30th, a couple's first years, a parent's 70th, a farewell from school. Here are memory book ideas by occasion, page prompts that make writing easy, and how to build one online for free so family in Perth and Brisbane can add to it too.

What to put in a memory book

  • Photos with a line of context — who, where and why it mattered.
  • Short stories: the night the car broke down, the first flat, the holiday that went wrong.
  • Voices — a recorded message or a laugh says more than a caption.
  • Lists: favourite songs, inside jokes, places you've been together.
  • Messages from other people who know the person.

Memory book ideas for friends and best friends

  • One chapter per year of friendship, starting with the first photo you have together.
  • “Things only we understand” — a page of inside jokes with the photo that started each one.
  • A road trip chapter with a voice note telling the story of the trip.
  • For a birthday: one photo and one memory from each friend in the group.

Memory book ideas for couples

  • How we met, first date, first holiday, first home — one chapter each.
  • “Then and now” pairs: the same place, years apart.
  • Letters to each other recorded as voice notes and placed next to the photos.
  • An anniversary chapter you add to every year.

Memory book ideas for parents and grandparents

Milestone birthdays and anniversaries are perfect for a family memory book. Ask each grandchild for one photo and one sentence. Better still, record Grandma telling the story behind an old photo — her voice next to the picture is the page everyone returns to.

Memory book ideas for kids and students

  • A school-year book: first day, sports carnival, excursions, the last day.
  • A farewell book for a teacher, with a message from every student.
  • A “my year” book the child narrates in their own voice.

Memory books for someone who has passed away

A memory book can help a family grieve together. Collect photos from relatives, add the stories people tell at the wake, and keep it private or shared only with family.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put in a memory book?

Photos with a line of context, short stories, lists of shared favourites, messages from friends and family, and if possible recorded voices.

How do you make a memory book for a friend?

Pick a structure — by year, place or theme — gather photos from the group, add one memory per photo and invite another friend to help as co-author.

Can I make a memory book online for free?

Yes. Story albums on Albums are free, with unlimited albums, chapters, voice notes and no ads. Free photo uploads are up to 30 MB per file.

Can family members view it without an account?
