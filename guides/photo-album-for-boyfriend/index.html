Photo album for your boyfriend: ideas for a gift he'll actually open

A photo album is one of the few gifts that gets better the longer you've been together. But a printed book takes weeks and most of your best photos are on your phone anyway. Here are photo album ideas for your boyfriend — for a birthday, an anniversary or Christmas — and how to turn them into an online album with your own voice that he can open from a link.

What to put in it

  • Your first photo together and the story of how you met.
  • First date, first trip, first Christmas — the “firsts” always work.
  • Inside jokes: the pub quiz you lost, the train you missed, the takeaway order you always get.
  • Places that matter: your local, the beach in Brighton, the city break in Edinburgh.
  • Screenshots of your first messages, if you dare.
  • Videos: short clips say more than any caption.

Ways to structure the album

  • Timeline: one chapter per year or per season of your relationship.
  • “Reasons I love you”: one photo with one reason per page.
  • Places: one chapter for every trip or city.
  • Birthday edition: one chapter per birthday you've spent together.

Caption ideas

  • “The day you pretended you knew the way.”
  • “Still my favourite person to do nothing with.”
  • “Proof we were cute before the coffee.”

Add your voice

A short voice note on the best photos — how you felt that day, what he said — turns a slideshow into a gift. He can play the album as a slideshow with your narration.
