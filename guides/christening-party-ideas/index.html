Christening party ideas: from the church to the reception, with every guest's photos in one place

Searches for “christening party ideas” in the UK split into the same few questions: where to hold the reception, what food to serve, how to decorate for a baby girl or boy, and how to keep the day simple with a little one in tow. Here are ideas that work for a village hall, a pub function room or your own living room — plus an easy way to collect the photos and videos your guests take.

Where to hold the christening reception

  • At home: the easiest option with a baby — nap times and nappy changes are close at hand.
  • A pub function room: many pubs near the church offer a room and a buffet, so nobody has to cook.
  • A village or church hall: cheap to hire and big enough for children to run around.

Food that suits a christening

  • Afternoon tea: finger sandwiches, scones with jam and clotted cream, and mini cakes — easy to eat standing up.
  • A grazing table or buffet: sausage rolls, quiche, cheese and fruit for all ages.
  • The christening cake: a traditional fruit cake or a simple sponge with the baby's name; some families still keep the top tier of their wedding cake for this day.

Decorations for a baby girl or boy

Soft balloon garlands, white flowers, a welcome sign with the baby's name and the date, and a table with the christening gown or a framed photo from the service. Keep it light — the focus is the baby and the people around them.

Moments to remember

  • Godparents: a photo with each godparent and a short note from them about their promise.
  • Four generations: if great-grandparents are there, gather everyone for one photo together.
  • Wishes for the baby: a card or jar for messages that the child can read when they are older.
