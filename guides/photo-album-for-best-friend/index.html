Photo album for your best friend: a birthday gift full of your story

Look up “photo album for best friend” in Australia and you'll mostly find personalised photo books. They're great, but they can't hold the video from your road trip down the coast or your voice retelling the night you missed the last train. An online story album can — and you can make it tonight for free.

What to put in a best friend photo album

  • How you met: the school photo, uni orientation, the first job where you sat next to each other.
  • Big trips: Schoolies, a road trip, a weekend camping, the festival where it rained the whole time.
  • Everyday stuff: brunch spots, beach days, the group chat moments worth keeping.
  • Milestones: 18th and 21st birthdays, moving out, weddings, new babies.
  • Messages from others: a few lines from family or friends who know you both.
  • One caption per photo: inside jokes are the best part.
