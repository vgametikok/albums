Ugly sweater party ideas

Americans search “ugly sweater party ideas”, “ugly sweater party games”, “decorations” and “food” every December. Here is a simple plan for a home or office holiday party, from the invitation to the contest – and an easy way to get everyone's photos afterwards.

Invitations

  • Say “ugly sweater required” in the first line, so nobody shows up in a plain sweater.
  • Add the date, time and whether it's potluck or BYOB.
  • Mention the sweater contest and any prize – it gets people to make an effort.

Decorations

  • Tinsel, string lights and a garland of old sweaters or mini paper sweaters.
  • A small table with fake snow, a Santa hat and props for group photos.
  • Red, green and plaid tablecloths – the tackier, the better.

Food and drinks

  • Easy finger food: sliders, pigs in a blanket, a cheese board, chili in a slow cooker.
  • Sugar cookies decorated like sweaters with icing.
  • Hot cocoa bar with marshmallows and candy canes, eggnog or a holiday punch.

Games

  • Ugly sweater contest: categories like ugliest, most creative, best DIY and best couple.
  • White elephant gift exchange with a price limit, for example $20–25.
  • Holiday trivia or name-that-Christmas-song.
  • Sweater runway: everyone walks once, guests vote with their phones or by applause.

Prizes

  • A tacky trophy, a gift card or a silly crown for the winner.
  • Keep a small prize for the best DIY sweater.

Frequently asked questions

What do you do at an ugly sweater party?

Eat, drink, play a few holiday games and finish with an ugly sweater contest and a white elephant gift exchange.

What are good ugly sweater contest categories?

Ugliest, most creative, best DIY, funniest and best couple or group.

What food should I serve?

Easy finger food like sliders, a cheese board, chili and sweater-shaped sugar cookies, plus a hot cocoa bar.

How do I get photos from all the guests?

Share one QR code: guests upload photos and videos to the same album in the browser, and you can approve them first.
